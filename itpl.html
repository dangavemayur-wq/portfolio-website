About IndianOil Total Pvt. Ltd. (ITPL)

IndianOil Total Private Limited (ITPL) is a 50:50 joint venture between Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) and TotalEnergies. ITPL manufactures and markets high-quality Bitumen Derivatives, Polymer Modified Bitumen (PMB) including Styrelf®, Bitumen Emulsions, and Crumb Rubber Modified Bitumen (CRMB) to enhance road construction durability and infrastructure across India.
