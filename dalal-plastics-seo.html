Executive Performance Summary

  • Organic Traffic: Organic Search remained a strong and stable contributor in July, increasing from 538 to 583 users, while Direct traffic improved from 289 to 358 users, indicating steady organic visibility.
  • Improved Engagement: Bounce rate decreased from 44.76% to 41.81% and average time on page increased from 54 seconds to 57 seconds, indicating better-quality and more engaged visitors.
  • Google Ads Recovery: July performance was temporarily affected by a payment issue, reducing clicks to 227 and impressions to 35,632. Post-resolution, the search campaign was restructured ad group-wise to refine keywords and ad relevance.
  • LinkedIn Social Engagement: Total follower count grew to 6,069. Post engagement saw a major boost, climbing to 7,912 engagements (+85% MoM growth).
